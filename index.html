BMW F01 LEGACY

The art of engineering. The legacy of luxury.

Brief History and Timeline

Unveiled in Moscow in July 2008, followed by the Paris Motor Show in October 2008. Styled by Karim Habib under the direction of chief designer Adrian van Hooydonk, moving away from the controversial styling of the preceding E65 generation toward a more traditional, clean aesthetic.

Platform

Built on the BMW L6 platform, which also underpinned the Rolls-Royce Ghost.

Facelift(LCI)

The coming of the Life Cycle Impulse update in 2012 for the 2013 model year brought updated LED headlights, revised bumpers, an upgraded iDrive system, and a standard 8-speed ZF automatic transmission.

End of the F01 Cycle

The F01 did a good run until July 2015, before being succeeded by the G11 generation which was a very very very beatiful predecessor to the marvellous F01.
